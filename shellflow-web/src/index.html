MR11 Mobile

Mobile App for Critical & Urgent Inputs

An installable web app with the normal sign-in and role checks. It caches only the app shell, never MR11 data, so nobody sees old figures.

  • Site progress: dates, m² and tower/block, with a photo from the camera.
  • Panel actuals: daily production by panel code, replacing WhatsApp actuals.
  • Urgent payment: payment landed; goes to Finance to confirm.
  • Dispatch check: is this row green to load, plus freight confirmation.
  • Alerts: open alerts and due reminders.

Every entry is timestamped and shows as "Mobile" in the update log on the Approvals & Log tab.
